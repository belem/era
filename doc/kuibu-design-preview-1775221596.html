Design System — Traditional Chinese Colors

The thesis: A poetry app rooted in traditional Chinese color. Rice paper warmth, pine green for authority, jade for growth, crabapple pink for delight. Every color is drawn from classical Chinese pigments. The palette feels alive because each hue carries centuries of meaning.
